chubbo privacy policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

chubbo is operated by Shavaiz Khan. Contact: shavaiz_khan@outlook.com.

Your game stays on your device

chubbo stores your chubbo, room, outfit, Dream Coins, puzzle and saved gameplay progress, sleep check-ins and related gameplay state on your device. Friends sign-in does not back up your full game. Removing the app may remove your saved progress.

Apple Health and sleep

With your permission, chubbo reads steps, walking distance and sleep from Apple Health to update activity progress, rest and an optional daily puzzle hint. It does not write to Apple Health. Raw HealthKit samples are processed on your device and are not uploaded by the app. Derived gameplay state, such as your chubbo's form, can reflect your activity and sleep. Manual sleep entry and check-ins are available; a check-in estimates time in bed rather than independently measuring sleep.

With background step support enabled, iOS may allow chubbo to read and cache a dated cumulative step total while the app is closed. A protected device-local cache helps refresh the game on return; it is not a raw Health sample archive. Background execution and access to locked Health data depend on iOS.

You can change Health permissions in iOS. Health access is optional. chubbo is a game, not a medical service, and is not intended to diagnose or treat conditions.

Friends and optional step sharing

Friends uses Sign in with Apple and Supabase for authentication and hosted social data. The authentication service processes your Apple account identifier and any information Apple supplies during that authentication flow. chubbo does not ask for or receive your Apple password.

The Friends service stores an account profile, companion name, friend code, requests, friendships, blocks, sharing preferences and a room postcard. The postcard includes your chubbo's species, colour, form, equipped accessories, its latest shared facial expression (which may reflect tiredness), and up to twelve decorations with their positions. Accepted friends can see that postcard. Pending requests show companion names. There is no public nearby-player directory.

When you link your game to Friends, daily step totals, local dates, timezone and observation times are sent to Supabase. Where weekly standings are enabled, completed days from the current week are also read to fill missed syncs. Accepted friends can see your daily and current-week totals and rankings. Older daily observations are pruned on subsequent syncs. Using Friends is optional; full HealthKit records, sleep history and walking-distance readings are not sent to Friends. Your visible companion form can also reflect gameplay progress. Turning step sharing off removes the shared step entries from the active database; other users see the change when they refresh.

Where enabled, background step sharing uses a limited device credential stored in the device Keychain; the server keeps its hash, an installation identifier, expiry and last observation time. It can upload only current-day step totals for its account and does not grant access to profiles or sleep data. The credential expires after 30 days and can renew when you use the app. Signing out clears it locally and attempts server revocation. Disabling sharing revokes device credentials; account deletion removes them.

You can remove or block friends, sign out, or delete your Friends account from Friends → My code. Account deletion removes the active hosted account and its social records, not your local game or Apple Health records. You can also manage chubbo's Sign in with Apple authorization through your Apple account settings. Contact us if you cannot use the in-app deletion flow.

Private friend notes and form notifications

Shared names and private notes are checked for common abusive wording. Automated checks are limited and do not replace reporting, blocking or operator review. Report an inappropriate note in-app or contact support about a name or other concern.

Where enabled, accepted friends can send a private note of up to 80 characters after a recorded regression into Round or Extra Round within the past 36 hours, while the recipient remains in that form or rounder. Supabase stores the note, sender and recipient identifiers, timestamps and read state. Only the participants' permitted account actions expose notes; they do not appear on public postcards or leaderboards. Removing or blocking a friend stops new notes and hides their notes from the normal inbox. Reported notes are hidden and retained for operator review. Other notes expire at the recipient’s next local midnight and are removed by the scheduled worker. Minimal send IDs and timestamps remain for 30 days to enforce daily limits and safe retries. Account deletion removes related notes and reports from the active database.

You can control receipt of private notes, friend form alerts and sharing of form updates in Settings → Friends and notes. A confirmed change into a rounder game form can create an update for accepted friends; no additional Health samples or measured body weight are collected for this feature. First sync does not create a regression alert. Eligible updates are grouped into one daily digest at noon in your local timezone. Note alerts use grouping, cooldowns and overnight quiet hours.

If you enable push notifications, the service stores your Expo push token, installation identifier, platform and timezone. Expo and Apple or Google process notification delivery. Lock-screen note alerts name the sender but do not include the private message text. Delivery tickets and error codes support troubleshooting; notification-job metadata is removed after 30 days and inactive push registrations after 90 days. Device notification settings can prevent display.

App referrals and puzzle keepsakes

If you use a referral code, the service stores the referring account, the new account, the linking time and whether the new account completed its first puzzle. A new account can link one referral within seven days, before its first completed puzzle. The referring player receives 100 game coins for each qualifying account and one Buddy Arcade keepsake. Sharing alone does not earn rewards. The service keeps reward receipts to prevent repeated claims; users cannot see another account’s private reward ledger.

Puzzle streak keepsakes use dated completions. Your account can restore up to 400 recent completion dates; your local game keeps reward receipts and earned items. A completed puzzle means solved or all six guesses used. These rewards do not send messages or collect contacts. Opening the share sheet lets you choose who receives your invite. Referral and reward records are tied to account deletion controls; already granted local game coins and items remain on-device.

Contact invitations

If you choose Invite from contacts, chubbo asks permission to show contact names and phone numbers on your device. It reads only your authorized contacts, including the limited selection allowed by iOS. The address book is not uploaded, matched against a server directory or kept in app storage. You choose a person and review a prefilled message in Messages before sending. Nothing is sent automatically. The message contains your invite link and friend code; the recipient must have chubbo installed to open the link. You can share a link without contacts access and can change contacts permissions in iOS Settings.

Home Screen widget

If you add the optional iPhone widget, chubbo shares a small device-local snapshot with its widget extension: your character appearance, a dated daily step total and your last recorded sleep duration. The shared container does not include your name, full game save, raw Health samples, address book or Friends credentials. The widget does not upload this snapshot. Its derived cache is excluded from normal backup and marked as privacy-sensitive for supported system appearances. iOS controls widget visibility and refresh timing. You can remove the widget from your Home Screen; the app's step refresh does not require a widget.

Feedback and crash reports

Written feedback is sent to Sentry when you submit it. It includes your message, category, report ID and basic app metadata. Please leave out personal or Health information. Unsent feedback drafts are stored locally. Share report opens your phone's share sheet; the destination you choose receives that copy.

Automatic crash reporting is a separate option and defaults off. If enabled, Sentry receives technical error diagnostics, including app release and stack information. JavaScript error messages are replaced with generic error text, and JavaScript events are filtered to remove account context, arbitrary extra data and breadcrumbs. Native crash reports may include technical device and runtime diagnostics.

The app disables automatic screenshots, session replay, console-log collection, view-hierarchy capture and performance tracing. It does not deliberately attach Health records, game saves or authentication tokens to crash reports. Turn off Share crash reports in Settings to stop future automatic reporting. Written feedback remains a separate action.

Reminders

Optional local bedtime and wake reminders use the routine you choose. They do not measure sleep. Disable them in the app or iOS notification settings.

Service providers and storage

Supabase provides authentication and Friends storage. Sentry receives submitted feedback and opted-in diagnostics. Apple provides Sign in with Apple, HealthKit and TestFlight distribution/feedback. These providers may process information outside your country under their applicable service and privacy terms.

Local game data and drafts remain until removed through the app's applicable controls or device storage removal. Hosted account/social records are retained while the account is active, subject to deletion controls. Older shared step entries are pruned on later step updates; this is not a guaranteed timed expiry for an inactive account. Submitted feedback and diagnostics are retained as needed to investigate problems and improve the app, subject to the providers' configured retention. Service logs and backups may remain for their applicable retention periods after active records are removed.

Contact shavaiz_khan@outlook.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of hosted information, or to ask about the retention settings that apply to a report. We may need enough information to locate and verify the relevant account or report. Do not send Apple passwords, private tokens or Health screenshots.

chubbo does not use Health information for advertising or data brokerage. The app does not include a separate behavioral analytics SDK or advertising SDK.

Policy updates

Health access, Friends step sharing and automatic crash reporting are optional. You can stop using these features at any time. We will update this policy when our data practices change and notify affected testers of material changes.
