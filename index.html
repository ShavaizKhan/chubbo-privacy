chubbo privacy policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

chubbo is operated by Shavaiz Khan. Contact: shavaiz_khan@outlook.com.

Your game stays on your device

chubbo stores your companion, room, outfit, Dream Coins, adventure progress, sleep check-ins and related gameplay state on your device. Friends sign-in does not back up your full game. Removing the app may remove your saved progress.

Apple Health and sleep

With your permission, chubbo reads steps, walking distance and sleep from Apple Health to update your companion and adventures. It does not write to Apple Health. Raw HealthKit samples are processed on your device and are not uploaded by the app. Derived gameplay state, such as your companion's form, can reflect your activity and sleep. Manual sleep entry and check-ins are available; a check-in estimates time in bed rather than independently measuring sleep.

You can change Health permissions in iOS. Health access is optional. chubbo is a game, not a medical service, and is not intended to diagnose or treat conditions.

Friends and optional step sharing

Friends uses Sign in with Apple and Supabase for authentication and hosted social data. The authentication service processes your Apple account identifier and any information Apple supplies during that authentication flow. chubbo does not ask for or receive your Apple password.

The Friends service stores an account profile, companion name, friend code, requests, friendships, blocks, sharing preferences and a room postcard. The postcard includes your companion's species, colour, form, equipped accessories, and up to four decorations with their positions. Accepted friends can see that postcard. Pending requests show companion names. There is no public nearby-player directory.

Daily step sharing starts off. If you enable it, your current-day step total, local date, timezone and observation time are sent to Supabase. Accepted friends can see your total and ranking. This is an optional upload of Health-derived information; full HealthKit records, sleep history and walking-distance readings are not sent to Friends. Your visible companion form can also reflect gameplay progress. Turning step sharing off removes the shared step entries from the active database; other users see the change when they refresh.

You can remove or block friends, sign out, or delete your Friends account from Friends → My code. Account deletion removes the active hosted account and its social records, not your local game or Apple Health records. You can also manage chubbo's Sign in with Apple authorization through your Apple account settings. Contact us if you cannot use the in-app deletion flow.

Feedback and crash reports

Written feedback is sent to Sentry when you submit it. It includes your message, category, report ID and basic app metadata. Please leave out personal or Health information. Unsent feedback drafts are stored locally. Share report opens your phone's share sheet; the destination you choose receives that copy.

Automatic crash reporting is a separate option and defaults off. If enabled, Sentry receives technical error diagnostics, including app release and stack information. JavaScript error messages are replaced with generic error text, and JavaScript events are filtered to remove account context, arbitrary extra data and breadcrumbs. Native crash reports may include technical device and runtime diagnostics.

The app disables automatic screenshots, session replay, console-log collection, view-hierarchy capture and performance tracing. It does not deliberately attach Health records, game saves or authentication tokens to crash reports. Turn off Share crash reports in Settings to stop future automatic reporting. Written feedback remains a separate action.

Reminders

Optional local bedtime and wake reminders use the routine you choose. They do not measure sleep. Disable them in the app or iOS notification settings.

Service providers and storage

Supabase provides authentication and Friends storage. Sentry receives submitted feedback and opted-in diagnostics. Apple provides Sign in with Apple, HealthKit and TestFlight distribution/feedback. These providers may process information outside your country under their applicable service and privacy terms.

Local game data and drafts remain until removed through the app's applicable controls or device storage removal. Hosted account/social records are retained while the account is active, subject to deletion controls. Older shared step entries are pruned on later step updates; this is not a guaranteed timed expiry for an inactive account. Submitted feedback and diagnostics are retained as needed to investigate problems and improve the beta, subject to the providers' configured retention. Service logs and backups may remain for their applicable retention periods after active records are removed.

Contact shavaiz_khan@outlook.com to ask about access, correction or deletion of hosted information, or to ask about the retention settings that apply to a report. We may need enough information to locate and verify the relevant account or report. Do not send Apple passwords, private tokens or Health screenshots.

chubbo does not use Health information for advertising or data brokerage. This beta does not include a separate behavioral analytics SDK or advertising SDK.

This beta and policy updates

The initial invite-only pilot is intended for adults. Participation, Health access, Friends step sharing and automatic crash reporting are optional. You can stop participating at any time. We will update this policy when our data practices change and notify affected testers of material changes.
