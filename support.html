chubbo support

Need help with chubbo? Email shavaiz_khan@outlook.com. Include your app version, iPhone model, and a short description of what happened. Do not send Apple passwords, authentication tokens, private notes, or screenshots containing Health records.

Apple Health and wearable sleep

chubbo reads supported data from Apple Health with your permission. A wearable must first write its sleep to Apple Health; data available only in the wearable’s own app cannot be read by chubbo. Check that the night appears in Apple Health → Sleep and chubbo has Sleep read permission, then reopen chubbo or use Refresh Apple Health in Settings. You can record sleep manually from Home → Sleep. Reminders do not measure sleep. Background updates depend on iOS.

Daily puzzles

Crosswire and One Lie alternate daily, with six guesses. Puzzles reset at midnight Eastern time, including daylight-saving changes. Tap the help button in Chubble? for examples. A completed puzzle means solving it or using all six guesses for streak purposes.

Accounts and lost progress

Friends sign-in does not back up the entire local game. Removing the app can remove local progress. To delete your hosted account, open Friends → My code → Delete Friends account and confirm with Apple. This does not delete your Apple Health records or local game. If deletion fails, contact support without sending credentials.

Referrals

During setup, optionally enter your friend’s referral code. You can also add it through Friends → Add code or Settings → Invite & earn. Sign in to link it before your first completed puzzle and within seven days of creating your Friends account. Finish that first puzzle to qualify your friend for 100 coins and the Buddy Arcade keepsake. Sharing alone does not earn a reward. Your friend must install chubbo from its App Store listing.

Safe, friendly notes

Keep notes encouraging. Harassment, threats, hateful language, sexual content, personal information, and body-shaming are not welcome. Notes refer to fictional game characters; chubbo does not measure a person’s body weight.

Report an inappropriate note using its Report action. You can block a person from their Friends postcard and turn off notes or form alerts in Settings → Friends and notes. For an inappropriate character name or any other safety concern, contact support with the friend code and a description, without sharing Health information. Reports are reviewed by the app operator; automated text checks are only a first layer of protection.
